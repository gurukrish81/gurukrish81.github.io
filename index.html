How I Think

Three principles that guide every engagement

01

"Fix the operational pain before you ask for organizational adoption." (Transformation)

AI strategy fails when it ignores systemic bottlenecks. True transformation doesn't happen by mandating model usage; it happens when you partner with Finance, align cross-functional OKRs, and build the clear financial impact models that prove value from day one.

02

"Governance that people bypass is just overhead. The gateway must be easier than the workaround." (Security & Governance)

Security can't be a roadblock. Safe deployment at scale happens when risk tiering and automated guardrails (aligned to NIST AI RMF and OWASP) are built directly into the path of least resistance, so engineers naturally choose compliance over workarounds.

03

"Architect the platform to scale; keep the operational headcount lean." (Systems)

Durable pipelines rely on smart system architecture over manual effort. Automating intake triaging and model-reuse patterns via intelligent orchestration workflows (n8n/LangGraph) keeps the core team lean while smoothly governing enterprise-wide scope.
